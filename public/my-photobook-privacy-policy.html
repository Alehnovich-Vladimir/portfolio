Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application My PhotoBook handles information when you use the app to create photo albums, digital books, printable photobooks, and PDF exports.

Core principle: My PhotoBook is designed to work locally on your device. The app does not require an account, does not use a developer-operated backend server, and does not upload your photos or album content to our own server.

1. About My PhotoBook

My PhotoBook allows users to create personal photo albums from photos selected on their device. Users can add album titles, subtitles, photo captions, cover images, page styles, colors, crop settings, photo order, and other album layout preferences.

The app can also create printable photobook layouts and generate PDF files that users may preview, open, save, or share through the standard system options available on their device.

2. Information processed by the app

Depending on how you use the app, My PhotoBook may process the following information locally on your device:

  • photos selected by you from your device photo library;
  • album names, subtitles, descriptions, captions, and other text entered by you;
  • album type, theme, color, cover style, layout settings, crop settings, and photo order;
  • copies of selected photos stored inside the app's private local storage;
  • photo metadata, such as the date and time when a photo was taken;
  • embedded photo location metadata, such as GPS coordinates, if such metadata exists in the selected photo and the feature is used;
  • generated PDF files created by the app at your request.

3. Photo access and media permissions

My PhotoBook may request access to your photo library so that you can choose photos for your albums. The app only uses the photos that you select for creating and editing your albums.

On supported devices, the app may also access embedded media metadata from selected photos. This can include the original photo date or embedded photo location information stored inside the image file.

4. Location information

My PhotoBook does not request or track your live device location.

Some photos may contain embedded location metadata, for example GPS coordinates saved by the camera at the time the photo was taken. If you use smart album features or location-based album organization, the app may read this embedded photo metadata to help group photos, generate album details, or display place names.

When coordinates from photo metadata are converted into readable place names, the device or platform geocoding services may be used. This processing is controlled by the operating system and platform services, not by a developer-operated My PhotoBook server.

5. Local storage

Album data and imported photo copies are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. This may include album information, selected photos, captions, cover settings, crop settings, print layout settings, and generated files.

My PhotoBook does not provide cloud synchronization and does not store your albums on a developer-operated server.

6. PDF generation, export, and sharing

My PhotoBook can generate PDF files from your albums. These PDF files may contain selected photos, captions, album titles, page layouts, and other album content chosen by you.

When you choose to preview, open, save, export, or share a PDF, the app may pass the file to Android system dialogs or to another app selected by you. After you choose another app or service, the handling of that file is governed by the privacy policy and settings of that selected app or service.

7. Data collection by the developer

The developer of My PhotoBook does not collect, sell, rent, or share your photos, albums, captions, or generated PDF files through a developer-operated backend server.

The app does not require user registration and does not require you to create an account.

8. Advertising and analytics

My PhotoBook does not include advertising SDKs.

My PhotoBook does not include developer-operated analytics SDKs for tracking user behavior inside the app.

9. Third-party services

My PhotoBook is built using mobile platform technologies and system services. Certain actions, such as selecting photos, opening files, sharing PDFs, or converting embedded photo coordinates into readable place names, may involve operating system services or apps selected by the user.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party apps or services that you choose to use for opening, saving, printing, or sharing exported files.

10. Data deletion

You can delete albums inside the app. Deleting an album removes the album information managed by the app.

You can also delete app data by clearing the app's storage through Android system settings or by uninstalling the app from your device.

Because My PhotoBook does not store your albums on a developer-operated server, the developer cannot remotely access or delete album data stored locally on your device.

11. Data security

My PhotoBook stores album data locally using the storage mechanisms available on your device. The security of locally stored data depends on your device, operating system, screen lock, backups, and other system-level security settings.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a secure lock screen and keeping your operating system updated.

12. Children

My PhotoBook is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated server. Photos and albums created in the app remain under the control of the device user.

Parents or guardians should supervise how children use photos, sharing features, and exported PDF files.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or platform requirements. When we update the policy, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
