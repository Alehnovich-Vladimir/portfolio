Privacy Policy for My Route

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Route handles information when you use the mobile application.

1. Developer and contact

My Route is provided by the developer of the My Route application. For privacy-related questions, you can contact us at: alexvovan.dev@gmail.com.

2. Information the app may access or process

My Route is a travel and route-planning application. Depending on the features you use, the app may access or process the following information:

  • Approximate or precise location: used to detect your current city, calculate distances, improve route navigation, and show nearby places.
  • Photos selected by you: used only when you choose to attach photos to visited places in your travel album.
  • Travel route and album data: such as selected cities, visited places, notes, saved trips, route progress, rewards, app language, theme, and local app preferences.
  • Network request data: when the app requests place information, map data, route information, translations, or city/place descriptions from external services.

3. How information is used

The information is used to provide the app's core features, including:

  • finding places and attractions near a selected city or your current location;
  • creating and displaying walking routes;
  • showing city and place information;
  • saving your routes, notes, photos, and travel memories locally on your device;
  • supporting app language, theme, audio guide, rewards, and map features.

4. Local storage

My Route stores route progress, saved trips, attached photos, rewards, app settings, and preferences locally on your device. This data is not used to create an account and is not sold.

5. Sharing with third-party services

My Route uses external services to provide maps, route calculation, place search, geocoding, city/place information, images, and translation features. Depending on the feature used, requests may be sent to services such as OpenStreetMap/Nominatim, Overpass API, OSRM, Photon/Komoot, Wikimedia/Wikipedia/Wikidata, Google Translate endpoints, Google Maps links, Carto/Esri map tiles, and the My Route backend proxy.

These services may receive technical request information such as coordinates, search queries, IP address, device/network information, and request metadata as necessary to respond to the app's requests. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Photos

The app accesses your photo library only after you grant permission and only when you choose to add photos to your travel memories. Selected photos are stored locally in the app's album data on your device. The app does not upload your selected photos to the My Route backend.

7. Location

The app requests location permission only when a feature needs it, such as detecting your current city, starting from your current location, improving navigation, or opening directions. Location data may be sent to mapping, geocoding, routing, or place-search services only to provide the requested feature.

8. Accounts, advertising, and payments

My Route does not currently require user registration or login. The app does not currently show third-party advertising. The current public release does not process in-app payments inside the app.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data saved locally by the app remains on your device until you delete it, reset app data, uninstall the app, or remove the saved content where the app provides such functionality. If you uninstall the app, locally stored app data may be deleted by the operating system.

10. Security

The app uses standard device storage and network communication mechanisms provided by the operating system and application framework. We take reasonable steps to limit data access to what is needed for the app's features.

11. Children's privacy

My Route is not specifically designed for children. The app is intended for a general travel audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
